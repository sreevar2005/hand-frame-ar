Hand Frame AR

Make a frame with your hands — a portal opens inside it.

Effect
Click “Start camera” to begin
Camera is off

How to use

  1. Press Start camera and allow camera access.
  2. Hold up both hands, palms toward the camera.
  3. Make an L with each hand: index finger up, thumb pointing in toward the other hand.
  4. Spread your hands apart. When the frame locks you'll see Frame detected ✓ and the portal appears.
  5. Move, tilt or stretch your hands — the portal follows.

Face filter: click Load face photo… and pick a clear, front-facing photo. When you frame your face with your hands, your face turns into the photo's face — only inside the frame. Use photos of yourself or people who have agreed.

🔒 Everything runs in this browser tab. Video is never recorded or uploaded.
